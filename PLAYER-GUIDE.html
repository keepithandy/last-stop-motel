Your first night

  1. Clean rooms 102 and 105. Select a room, then choose Clean. Each room uses one supply. You can do one task at a time.
  2. Repair room 105. Repairs cost $18 plus one supply, and return condition to 100. Wait for your work to finish.
  3. Open for the night. Travelers begin arriving soon. The game pauses on arrivals by default.
  4. Choose a traveler. Read their budget and preferences. Select a ready room, or use Find best room.
  5. Confirm the booking. A booking immediately adds its room price to your register. Happy guests add tips at sunrise.
  6. Answer requests. Gold markers above rooms mean someone needs a hand. Help before their patience runs out.
  7. Make decisions. Two events happen every night. Each includes an option that does not require money or supplies.
  8. Review the morning accounts. Everyone checks out. Clean, repair, and prepare for the next night.
A ready room needs at least 75 cleanliness and 35 condition.
Better condition, enough comfort, and a quiet location improve happiness. Match people to rooms instead of putting every guest in the most expensive one.

Know your room rates

Choose Value ($75), Standard ($100), or Premium ($130) in the Office. Add $25 for each quality level in a room. Guests will not book above their budget. Existing bookings keep their original rate.

Renovations cost $130, $190, then $270 for quality levels 1–3. They also restore cleanliness and condition. Rooms 104–110 face the courtyard; the others face the road. Soundproof windows remove the roadside penalty.

Staff need available supplies and repair money. Schedule shifts before opening. Only active staff receive that night’s wage. Team energy below 25 slows tasks by 30%; meals and morning rest restore it.

Spend with the sunrise in mind

More rooms mean more guests, but every expansion uses cash needed for the debt. Coffee and the garden improve happiness. Better laundry and tools speed up work. The neon sign brings one additional traveler each night.

Buy six supplies for $45 or twelve for $82. A supplier can extend credit once per night: four supplies add $45 to the campaign debt. In endless mode, credit is paid at the next sunrise. The optional emergency loan gives you $350 and adds $450 to the campaign debt.

Switch layouts

Open Settings and turn on Compact mobile layout for four tabs, one panel at a time, and lighter graphics. Turn it off to restore the full layout. Your choice is remembered, and you can switch during the same campaign.

Control the pace

A night runs from 18:00 to 02:00 and then jumps to sunrise. Normal speed advances three game minutes per real second. Choose 1×, 3×, or 6×. Menus, decisions, and the hidden browser tab pause the simulation.

Keep your progress

Automatic saves stay on your current browser and device. Export your campaign in Settings before switching browsers, moving files, or clearing browsing data. Import the JSON backup wherever you want to continue.

If the current automatic save is damaged, the game tries the previous automatic copy. If browser storage is unavailable, the game explains how to export a save manually.

If the 3D view has trouble

Use a current browser with WebGL 2 enabled. Lower Graphics quality in Settings on constrained hardware. The room register still exposes all management actions if the 3D renderer cannot start. For mobile play, use a hosted copy; local iOS file previews are not a reliable game browser.
