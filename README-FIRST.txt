LAST STOP MOTEL — VERSION 1.0.2

1. Extract the ENTIRE ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a current desktop browser.
3. Select New campaign.

No installation or internet connection is needed to play.
Keep index.html and the assets folder together.

Your goal: run seven nights, pay the final debt, and finish with
at least 40 reputation. Win to unlock endless mode.

The game includes a complete How to Play guide (H), three difficulty
settings, graphics/audio controls, automatic saving, and save export.

Phones/tablets: play from a static web host. iOS Files previews cannot
reliably run local HTML games. Touch controls are included; performance
has not been verified on physical devices.

To keep your progress when switching browsers or devices, open
Settings and export your save. Import that JSON on the new device.

COMPACT MODE: Settings > Compact mobile layout. Toggle it on or off
anytime. Your choice is remembered; your campaign is unchanged.
